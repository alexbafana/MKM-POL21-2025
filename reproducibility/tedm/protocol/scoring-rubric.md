# Pre-study scoring rubric

Status: draft requiring assessor freeze before data collection. No scores have been assigned.

For each relevant item: 0 = missing or materially incorrect; 1 = partly correct with a consequential omission; 2 = correct and source-grounded, with uncertainty retained; NA = inapplicable with reason. Record evidence locators, not just scores.

Criteria: goal/constraint linkage; material authority allocation; separation of ownership/voting/latent/exercised control; cross-pillar and temporal pathway correctness; distinction between documented premises and assumptions; treatment and residual risk; reconstructable monitoring; downstream semantic preservation; explicit representation gaps; internal contradictions.

A reference answer is an adjudicated source-based set of material findings, not the author's model treated as unquestionable truth. Allow valid alternative models and additional supported pathways. Two assessors independently score an initial subset, retain their pre-adjudication scores, revise the codebook before final scoring, and record disagreement resolution. Report task completion, unsupported claims, assistance, elapsed time, and unfavorable comparisons separately. Aggregate scores are descriptive and may conceal critical failures; publish criterion-level results.
