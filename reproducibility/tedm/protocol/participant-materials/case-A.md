# Constructed design vignette A

These facts define a researcher-constructed exercise, not observations of an operating DAO. All unstated facts are unknown. The study is artificial task evaluation, not evidence of deployed effectiveness.

A community aims to fund useful contributions while preserving meaningful participation in decisions. Its proposed token supply is capped. Founders receive 25% at launch, early funders 20%, contributors 25%, and treasury 30%. Founder and funder balances vest over two years, including a joint unlock in month twelve. Tokens are transferable. Eligible token holders may vote; voting weight multiplies balance by a lock-duration factor. Contributors can earn additional tokens; reward amounts are proportional to staked eligible balance. No simulation has yet been conducted.

Proposals enter a public forum. Two appointed moderators may filter them before a designated submitter advances them to voting. Rejection reasons and an appeal route are not specified. Delegation is allowed, but affiliations and common control are not recorded. A three-of-five signer group executes approved treasury transfers. Two signers are founder representatives; identities of the remaining signers are undisclosed. An emergency role can pause execution, with no published expiry or audit procedure. A service provider operates the proposal interface. No operational voting history or concentration baseline is available.

Task: recommend a design using the assigned method, document relevant uncertainty, and submit the requested design records. You may choose alternative mechanisms and justify them. Do not invent missing facts or treat the proposed configuration as approved.
